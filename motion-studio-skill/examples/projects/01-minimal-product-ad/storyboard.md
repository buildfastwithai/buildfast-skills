# Storyboard - Lumen (minimal product ad)

Brief: "A 15 second minimal product advertisement for Lumen, a smart desk lamp, calm motion"
Type product-ad · style minimal · motion calm · 16:9 · 15 s @ 30 fps · ambient score (84 bpm)

Concept: the lamp is introduced as a single drawn line and then *switches on* - the product literally lights up the ad.
One idea per shot, >= 50% negative space, one warm accent (the light itself).

| # | Time | Purpose | Visual | Camera | Animation | Typography | Transition | Audio |
|---|------|---------|--------|--------|-----------|------------|------------|-------|
| 01 hook | 0-3.6 | establish | lamp as a line drawing, right third | slow push 4% | strokes draw on, staggered | "Light, / considered." line reveal, left | diagonal mask | pad, no drums |
| 02 glow | 3.6-7.2 | the product comes alive | head close-up (bleeds off frame) | push 5% | beam + bulb fade on (1.4 s), temperature sweep | "Adapts to your day." + 2700 K -> 6500 K counter | push up | click on switch-on |
| 03 features | 7.2-11 | proof | three facts in columns with hairline rules | slow pan | one fact per beat | 12 W / Touch / Aluminium + captions | 1 s dissolve | ticks on each fact |
| 04 cta | 11-15 | brand | lit lamp left, wordmark right | still | wordmark tracking tightens, tagline, url | Lumen / Designed for focus. / lumen.studio | - (hold) | chime, tail |

Stillness is deliberate in 04 (declared `hold`).
