# Storyboard - Motion is a language (kinetic typography)

Brief: "13.5 second kinetic typography in experimental style with snappy motion, square 1:1, about why motion is a language"
Type kinetic-typography · style experimental · motion snappy · 1:1 1080x1080 · 13.5 s @ 30 fps · glitch score at 124 bpm (beat = 0.484 s)

Concept: type is the image. Every phrase lands on the beat; layout, scale and move change every time; the density flips
between yellow and black; one near-empty beat ("Silence.") is the pause before the key line.
